Famous Forts of India

Here are some of the well-known forts of India and a little about each of them.

Red Fort

Location: Delhi

The Red Fort was built by Emperor Shah Jahan in the 17th century. It is made of red sandstone. The Prime Minister hoists the national flag here every Independence Day. It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Raigad Fort

Location: Raigad district, Maharashtra

Raigad was the capital of the Maratha Empire under Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj. He was crowned here in 1674. The fort is on a high hill and visitors can reach the top by steps or by ropeway.

Sinhagad Fort

Location: Near Pune, Maharashtra

Sinhagad is a hill fort known for the brave battle of Tanaji Malusare in 1670. It is a popular place for trekking and gives a nice view of the surrounding hills.

Agra Fort

Location: Agra, Uttar Pradesh

Agra Fort was a main residence of the Mughal emperors. It is made of red sandstone and has palaces, halls and gardens inside. It is close to the Taj Mahal and is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Mehrangarh Fort

Location: Jodhpur, Rajasthan

Mehrangarh is one of the largest forts in India and stands on a tall hill above Jodhpur. It was started by Rao Jodha in 1459. Inside there is a museum with old weapons, paintings and palaces.

Chittorgarh Fort

Location: Chittorgarh, Rajasthan

Chittorgarh is one of the biggest forts in India by area. It is known for the Vijay Stambh (Tower of Victory) and for the stories of Rajput bravery. It is part of the Hill Forts of Rajasthan World Heritage Site.
