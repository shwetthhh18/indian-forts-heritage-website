Heritage Places of India

These monuments show the art, religion and culture of ancient and medieval India.

Taj Mahal

Location: Agra, Uttar Pradesh

The Taj Mahal was built by Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal. It is made of white marble and stands on the banks of the Yamuna river. It is one of the New Seven Wonders of the World.

Ajanta Caves

Location: Near Aurangabad, Maharashtra

Ajanta has many caves cut out of rock by Buddhist monks long ago. The caves are famous for their old wall paintings and sculptures. It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Ellora Caves

Location: Near Aurangabad, Maharashtra

Ellora has Buddhist, Hindu and Jain caves in one place. The most famous is the Kailasa Temple, which is carved from a single huge rock.

Hampi

Location: Karnataka

Hampi was the capital of the Vijayanagara Empire. Today it has ruins of temples, markets and palaces near the Tungabhadra river. The Virupaksha Temple and the Stone Chariot are well known.

Sanchi Stupa

Location: Sanchi, Madhya Pradesh

The Great Stupa at Sanchi is one of the oldest stone structures in India. It was started by Emperor Ashoka in the 3rd century BCE. It is a Buddhist monument with beautifully carved gateways.

Konark Sun Temple

Location: Konark, Odisha

The Konark Sun Temple was built in the 13th century and is shaped like a giant chariot of the Sun God with stone wheels. It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
