Introduction

India has hundreds of forts and heritage places built by different kings and dynasties over thousands of years. Some forts were built on hills to protect kingdoms, while many monuments were built for religion, art or love. Today, they attract visitors from all over the world.

Why is Indian Heritage Important?

Heritage places show us how people lived, what they believed in and how skilled their builders and artists were. They help us understand our history and are a source of pride. If we protect them, future generations can also learn from them.
